About SS Maa Traders

Delivering high-grade coal across India with reliability, speed, and integrity.

SS Maa Traders – Powering Industries Through Reliable Coal Transportation.

SS Maa Traders is a trusted coal transportation company based in Egarkunr, Pani Tanki, Dhanbad, Jharkhand. With a strong presence in the local transportation industry, we are committed to delivering reliable, efficient, and timely coal transportation services across Jharkhand.
We specialize in supplying and transporting coal to thermal power plants, steel industries, mines, and various industrial sectors. Our focus is on quality service, safe transportation, customer satisfaction, and building long-term business relationships.
Driven by commitment and professionalism, SS Maa Traders aims to be one of the leading names in coal transportation, providing dependable logistics solutions that keep industries running smoothly.

  • Reliable Transportation – Dependable coal delivery services.
  • Industrial Supply – Serving thermal power plants, steel industries and mines.
  • Local Expertise – Strong transportation network across Jharkhand.
  • Customer Commitment – Focused on timely delivery and customer satisfaction.
